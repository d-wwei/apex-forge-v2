# High Risk Review

## Purpose

Challenge decisions where a confident mistake is expensive or irreversible.

## Method

1. State the safety claim.
2. Attack assumptions, ordering, identity, concurrency, and rollback.
3. Test the highest-impact failure paths.
4. Require Approval for unresolved material risk.

## Output

Produce `high-risk-evidence` with safety claim, assumptions, adversarial cases,
blast radius, rollback, approval need, and residual risks.
